## Structural differences

- **rep7 is organized directly by acquisition.** Its top-level folders encode date, time, timepoint, and substrate/sample (for example, `10.19.22_11AM_TP8_Chitin`).
- **rep1 is organized first by substrate.** `Chitin`, `Chito5`, and `NAG` contain their individual dated acquisition folders (for example, `Chitin/11.22.21_17`).
- **rep1 contains substantially more processed image products.** Its inventory includes `*_wo_bg.TIF` (background-removed) and `*_masked.TIF` files; rep7 has neither naming pattern.
- **rep7 contains nine `random_windows` subdirectories** (all three substrates at timepoints 8–10), with random/inoculation-window images, thumbnails, composites, and `.nd` files. This structure is absent from rep1.
- **Both replicates contain original multi-channel image series** (`w1SD_BF`, `w2SD_DAPI`, `w3SD_GFP`, `w4SD_RFP`), stitched images, masks, and `.nd` metadata files.
